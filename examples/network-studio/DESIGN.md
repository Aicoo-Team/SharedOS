## Design Context

### Users

SharedOS and Aicoo product builders, agent researchers, and operators who are
designing or launching multi-agent systems. The first job is to choose a network
model, select its entry agents, define completion policy, and understand the
network's live state at a glance.

### Brand Personality

Precise, orchestrated, ambitious. The product should feel like an experimental
control surface for serious agent systems rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, editorial operations console inspired by research workbenches and
printed systems diagrams. Warm paper surfaces, dense ink, and a restrained
signal-orange accent replace the common purple-blue neon AI aesthetic. English
display typography carries the product voice while Simplified Chinese remains
highly legible for working copy.

### Design Principles

1. Make network topology and completion state visible before exposing advanced configuration.
2. Separate local agent-turn completion from whole-network completion in the language and UI.
3. Use progressive disclosure: start with two network modes and a small set of decisive controls.
4. Treat motion as operational feedback, never decoration.
5. Keep the first prototype functional, responsive, and understandable without backend data.
